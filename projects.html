Featured Research Project

Delayed but Durable: Causal Evidence on Medicaid Expansion and Preventive Care, 2012–2024

Causal Inference · Public Health · Health Policy

This research investigates whether Medicaid expansion increased the use of preventive health services in the United States. Using large-scale Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System data, the study examines changes in preventive care across states and over time.

The analysis combines modern causal-inference approaches with machine-learning methods to study policy effects across several preventive health outcomes. The project emphasizes careful research design, dynamic treatment effects, reproducible statistical workflows, and the interpretation of policy-relevant findings.

Data Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS)
Study Period 2012–2024
Research Area Causal Inference & Health Policy
Computing R · Reproducible Workflow
